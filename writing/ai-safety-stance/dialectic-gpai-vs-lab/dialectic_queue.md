## Round 2 — QUEUED, PRIMARY CANDIDATE: teacher-engineer lane (Louis's selection)

From the Round 1 idea burst, four coherent directions emerged:

### Direction 1 (Louis's selection) — The teacher-engineer first-tier bet

The auditor's harder contradiction. Louis has **first-tier access** in a market neither monk explored: Sorbonne M2 (28 students/year, rated 16.5/20, direct contact with future French business leaders and decision-makers), 42 Network curriculum (25,000+ students across 50+ campuses). Combined with production ML + agentic-systems fluency + 42AI institutional history, this is counterfactually specific in a way GPAI or Kyutai cannot match.

- **Monk X belief:** The teacher-engineer lane dominates both GPAI and Kyutai on counterfactual-specificity grounds. Louis is not a second-tier integrator — he is a first-tier educator of the decision-makers who will actually determine AI governance uptake. The Round 1 synthesis underweights this because both monks were trained to see teaching as peripheral.
- **Monk Y belief:** Teaching is a distraction from the infrastructure work the 2026 moment demands. Educators don't ship verification demos. The teacher lane is real but non-load-bearing — it's legacy positioning Louis already has; the question is what he *builds* in the next 12 months, and teaching doesn't produce the artifact.

### Direction 2 (queued) — The non-commitment problem

Louis's explicit commitment: "deliver value for humanity" with agency over paths. Pierre wants trajectory alignment. The Round 1 synthesis reconciles these via rehearsal-commitment + concrete deliverables. But is this real commitment, or sophisticated non-commitment?

- Monk X: rehearsal-with-deliverables IS real commitment to the mission, separable from commitment to any specific career path
- Monk Y: "commitment to value, not to path" is non-commitment dressed in philosophical language; Louis ends up nowhere specific and the mission suffers

### Direction 3 (queued) — Dependency robustness

The Round 1 synthesis stacks external dependencies: Pierre's 10% overhead, ENS partnership, Abbaszadeh response, AVERI openness, Kyutai offer, EU AI Act enforcement, Coefficient Giving renewal. If 2–3 fail, does the pathway actually connect?

- Monk X: dependencies are what makes the bet real; each resolves at a specific gate
- Monk Y: multi-legged stool that breaks on any leg; Louis is underestimating compound failure probability

### Direction 4 (queued) — Personal sustainability vs. integrator bet

10–15 hrs/week frontier-adjacent + full-time Pierre's project + relationship matrix (AVERI, Kyutai, Abbaszadeh, Pierre, Coefficient Giving). Is this sustainable over 9 months for one person with a life (partner, family, community)?

- Monk X: 10–15 hrs/week is realistic for a driven engineer
- Monk Y: the integrator load is self-sacrifice masquerading as strategy
