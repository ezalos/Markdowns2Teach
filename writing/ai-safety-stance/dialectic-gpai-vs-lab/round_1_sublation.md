# Round 1 — Sublation (Aufhebung)

**Synthesis of the dialectic between Monk A (governance-technical hybrid career) and Monk B (frontier ML engineering career), following Phase 4 determinate negation and Boydian decomposition.**

---

## The reframe

**Louis is not choosing a career lane. He is instantiating a connective pathway between second-tier positions in two ecosystems that currently do not connect.**

Both monks argued *which tier* to commit to. Both were trapped in the same unit of analysis: Louis-as-agent, occupying-a-position, optimizing individual career capital over decades. Neither could see that Louis's actual strategic situation — no plausible access to first-tier positions in either direction (Anthropic/DeepMind visa-blocked or long-shot; AVERI already exists with $7.5M from the same funder as Louis's grant) but strong second-tier access in both (Kyutai ≥50% likely; GPAI offered) — makes *tier-internal competition structurally inferior to cross-tier pathway instantiation*.

The unit of analysis shifts from **position** to **pathway**. The objective function shifts from **individual career capital** to **counterfactual-specific contribution**, consistent with Louis's own stated value (*non nobis solum* — not for ourselves alone; "deliver value for humanity"). The temporal structure shifts from **leap commitment** (3 years or not-at-all) to **rehearsal commitment** — a bounded genuine engagement that generates information about whether to renew, with both ends of the pathway kept active during the formation period.

This is the move neither monk could produce from within their frames. Monk A optimized for position-taking in governance; Monk B optimized for position-taking in frontier. Neither had the connective vocabulary.

The "formation window" logic is not wrong, but applied to the wrong target: Louis is not currently inside the frontier paradigm Monk B warns him against leaving, so the logic must be applied to *something other than tier-internal commitment*.

## What is elevated — the new structure

### 1. Louis is a pathway-instance, not a position-taker

The corticospinal tract runs from cortex (decision/paradigm) to spinal cord (implementation/governance). It is neither — it is the connective infrastructure. It myelinates only when both ends are simultaneously active during a specific formation window. There are two pyramidal tracts — plurality of connective pathways, not a single true path.

The Louis-isomorphism: he is not the cortex (Anthropic-tier frontier paradigm-definer, structurally inaccessible) and he is not the spinal cord (AVERI-tier founding governance voice, already occupied). He is a candidate connective pathway instance — a French senior ML engineer with governance-adjacent institutional history (42AI, Sorbonne teaching), Pierre relationship, agentic-systems fluency, distributed-training experience, and the specific skill combination that bridges frontier capability and verification infrastructure. His unique value is not as *occupier* of either position but as *connector* between them.

### 2. Counterfactual-specificity is the objective function

Under *non nobis solum*, the measure of a move is not "how much does this grow Louis" but "how much of this only Louis can do." Applied:
- **Pierre's specific gap at GPAI (alone on application side, needs Louis-shaped engineer now, with Louis specifically accelerating the announced timeline): counterfactually specific to Louis.** Not any-Louis-shaped-person.
- **Kyutai's need for a senior ML engineer: not counterfactually specific.** Kyutai has multiple candidates; Louis is a good one among several.
- **AVERI exists as an ecosystem actor with different strengths:** not competitor, but potential pathway node Louis could connect to rather than replace.

This inverts Monk A's reasoning. The argument for GPAI is not "neglected field" (AVERI weakens this) — it is **counterfactual-specific fit to Pierre's specific gap at this exact moment**. This is more defensible, narrower, and honest about what Louis's value actually is.

### 3. Two-gate rehearsal commitment structure

The grant should be signed — but only after clearing a **pre-signing diligence gate**, and with a separate **post-signing renewal gate** at month 5. These are categorically different and conflating them breaks the structure: pre-signing evidence cannot be used as post-signing renewal criteria, or Louis ends up deciding renewal with 30% of the evidence he needs because the evidence arrives after the decision point.

**Pre-signing gate (weeks 1–3, resolves BEFORE Louis commits):**
- Pierre's 10% overhead claim survives direct technical scrutiny — specific protocol, specific cryptographic primitive (pure ZKP vs hybrid TEE+commitment), specific regime under which 10% holds. Not just verbal assurance.
- ENS France cryptography partnership named with specific researchers, specific papers in flight or submitted, and confirmation Louis will have direct collaborative contact with them.
- Abbaszadeh et al. response received: either "this is live research, collaboration of interest" (strongly validates) or "this is not where our work is pointing" (flags fundamental viability concern).
- GPAI Policy Lab funders named. Due diligence that Louis can defend to himself and to Coefficient Giving's reviewer.
- Co-authorship and open-source release rights confirmed **in writing** before signing — not "we'll work it out."
- **Pierre's real concern addressed.** Pierre is open to the 6+6 structure operationally (per Louis's testimony); Pierre's actual worry is that Louis rushes to frontier capabilities after GPAI and is counterproductive to the verification mission. The honest answer Louis can give: *his commitment is to deliver value for humanity, with multiple paths available and agency to choose among them.* Verification-into-frontier-labs is ONE credible post-GPAI path (it would literally distribute the work into the labs that need to adopt it), but Louis is not locking to it. The operational commitments Louis can make concretely — deliver the demo during the engagement, open-source before exit, honor publication rights — protect the mission regardless of trajectory. If Pierre finds that credible → gate clears. If Pierre needs a specific post-GPAI commitment that Louis cannot honestly give, that's legitimate pre-signing evidence of a trust-gap the synthesis cannot paper over.

**If any pre-signing gate fails, Louis declines the grant.** He does not "sign and hope." Declining at this stage costs almost nothing — the relationships with Pierre, Tom, Coefficient Giving, and AVERI remain intact; Louis pursues Kyutai/Pruna with full attention.

**Post-signing renewal gate (month 5, resolves DURING the engagement):**
- Scope has measurably settled — a named target model size, a named protocol, an honest technical roadmap that the ENS partners agree with.
- Co-authored paper trajectory real — either submitted or committed submission schedule.
- Louis's parallel frontier-adjacent work actually happened during months 1–5 (not just theoretically protected — actually executed).
- Kyutai/Pruna conversation has a concrete next-step signal (offer, follow-up interview, or explicit "we'd value you joining after GPAI").
- Louis's own felt sense: is the work deposit-worthy, or is he hedging in place?

**If the post-signing renewal gate clears at month 5, extend to 12 months.** If it doesn't, exit cleanly at month 6 with a portable artifact (at minimum: a comparative benchmark of ZKP vs TEE vs flexHEG for AI governance, co-authored with Pierre if possible — a smaller but real credential).

The key structural point: **the renewal decision must be made with evidence that can only exist inside the engagement** — which is why it's a post-signing gate, not a pre-signing one. Pre-signing diligence catches the cases where the engagement shouldn't start at all. Post-signing renewal catches the cases where it started but isn't producing what was promised.

### 4. Both ends of the pathway kept explicitly active — with a concrete time floor

Time is non-fungible. "Both ends active" as principle fails unless it has operational weight. During the rehearsal period, the pathway requires **roughly 10–15 hours/week of frontier-adjacent work** — not 40% (which Pierre would read as hedging during the pre-signing gate and reject), not 5% (which is atrophy, Monk B's prediction). The Goldilocks zone is ~25–30% of a focused work week, executed primarily on evenings/weekends plus one carved-out weekday half-day.

Concrete breakdown:
- **GPAI end (governance-technical, ~70–75%):** Pierre, ZKP demonstration, policy framing, institutional positioning. The funded work.
- **Frontier-adjacent end (capability, ~10–15 hours/week):**
  - 1–2 hours/week active Kyutai/Pruna relationship (sustained conversation, not check-ins)
  - 4–6 hours/week SOTA LLM training literature + distributed systems reading (Louis has indicated he'll do this anyway — now committed)
  - 2–3 hours/week independent agentic-systems work (Louis uses Claude Code actively — now explicitly tracked)
  - 1–2 hours/week AVERI/Brundage relationship during the active collaboration-exploration period
- **Cross-connecting move:** AVERI outreach is the one unambiguously novel recommendation of this synthesis. Same funder (Coefficient Giving), adjacent mission, different entry point. Whether Brundage frames GPAI as collaborator or competitor shapes Louis's month-5 renewal read.

The time floor is **contractual, not aspirational.** It goes into the pre-signing conversation with Pierre explicitly: *"My scope is verification-demo work; I will also maintain ~10–15 hours/week of distributed-training reading + Kyutai relationship + AVERI coordination. Is that compatible with what you need?"* Pierre's answer is itself pre-signing gate evidence.

The time floor is also **measurable at month-5 renewal.** Either Louis actually executed the 10–15 hours/week (pathway is forming) or Pierre's project consumed everything despite the pre-signing agreement (pathway isn't forming; renewal decision is informed by that fact).

### 5. Absorption is neutral — what matters is the deposit

Savoy Records isomorphism: independent-tier work builds decades of niche depth, and eventually gets absorbed (Concord, 2017). Absorption is not trap (Monk B) or ladder (Monk A) — it is what second-tier work does over decades. The question isn't "will Louis be absorbed" but "absorbed into what, contributing what." The synthesis is agnostic about where absorption lands (Pattern 2 governance career, re-entry to frontier via an open-source pathway artifact, founding-level AVERI-adjacent role, academic position, European sovereignty-aligned lab) — it insists that what Louis deposits during the pathway period determines the absorption terms.

## New contradictions this sublation generates (Phase 7 recursion candidates)

1. **The time-division problem.** Pathway-instantiation requires keeping both ends active, but Louis is one person with finite time. At what weighting does "both ends warm" become "neither end deep"? What's the minimum intensity per end for the pathway to form vs. for either end to remain viable?

2. **The Pierre-dependency problem.** Counterfactual-specific fit to Pierre's gap assumes Pierre's technical vision is correct. If Pierre's 12-month demo fails (cryptographic physics, team absence, funder withdrawal), what happens to Louis's pathway? Does the rehearsal-commitment exit at month 6 actually function if Pierre is in the middle of a crisis then?

3. **The re-entry information asymmetry.** At month 6, Louis faces a renewal decision with better information than today — but still binary. The rehearsal commitment structure provides *exit legibility* (clear decision point, clear contingencies) but doesn't eliminate the fundamental commitment. What does rehearsal-commitment look like when the renewal point has its own renewal point?

4. **Non-nobis-solum vs. personal sustainability.** The synthesis uses non-nobis-solum as objective function. But Louis has real constraints (financial runway, partner situation, family context not surfaced in interview). At what point does the integrator bet become self-sacrifice rather than contribution? The dialectic didn't interrogate this.

5. **AVERI asymmetry risk.** Outreach to Brundage might reveal AVERI is doing verification work more efficiently, or conversely that AVERI sees GPAI as a junior partner rather than collaborator. Both outcomes change Louis's optimal move. What's the update function if AVERI is dominant, and what if AVERI is welcoming?

These are real, fresh tensions. Fertility, not finality.
