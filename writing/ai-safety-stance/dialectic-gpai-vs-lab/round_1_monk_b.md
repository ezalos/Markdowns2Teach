# Round 1 — Monk B: The Frontier Engineering Advocate

**Position:** Louis's highest-leverage move in 2026 is to join a frontier ML lab directly — ideally Anthropic's safety team, secondarily Kyutai or Pruna. The GPAI move is a values-driven, friendship-weighted rationalization that will produce governance-engineering output and structurally cap his lifetime influence on safety. Decline the grant.

---

## a. The ontological claim

The 2026 moment for an ML engineer with Louis's skill stack is not a "career opportunity" — it is a **formation window**, and formation windows close. What is being formed is not a résumé but a *mode of technical intuition* that only accretes through hands-on contact with frontier-scale systems at the moment their paradigm is being invented. Louis cannot acquire agentic / RL-post-training / long-horizon-agent intuition from the outside — it is tacit, paradigm-bound, learned by watching your training runs fail in novel ways.

The proper structure of a career that serves safety at the highest leverage is **frontier-first, then authority-redirected**. Chris Olah built frontier reputation at Brain before interpretability became non-optional reading. Dario Amodei was Director of Research at OpenAI before Anthropic. Paul Christiano co-authored *Concrete Problems in AI Safety* as a frontier insider — RLHF is his because he was inside the paradigm that made it necessary. Jan Leike co-led superalignment at OpenAI before redirecting to Anthropic's scalable-oversight work in 2024. [1] **Technical authority is career-path-dependent.** Engineers who skip the formation window never fully acquire the tacit knowledge that lets them tell a real alignment concern from an eval artifact. Their voice in governance forums is always one rank below the frontier-formed. Louis believes he can enter governance first and redirect later. The data (§5) is unambiguous: **nobody has done this.** Technical authority does not regenerate outside the paradigm that produces it.

## b. Monk A's strongest case

Monk A's strongest claim: Louis's comparative advantage is **governance-technical hybrid work at a moment of genuine infrastructure neglect.** The 2025 Alignment Field Survey's total silence on ZKP-of-training is prima facie evidence of a gap no one is filling. Louis's skill stack — billion-scale production ML, distributed multi-GPU training, agentic systems fluency, teaching and leadership, French-European context — is unusually well-matched to this gap in ways frontier IC at Kyutai would not extract. Louis has agency as senior application lead to push scope toward 1B+, and if Pierre's 10% overhead holds via a hybrid TEE-commitment-selective-ZKP protocol, the credential is first-of-kind and career-defining. Pattern 2 destinations represent *optionality pull* — better senior roles than frontier IC gives Louis-shaped people. First serious voices in emerging governance subfields shape decades of downstream practice. The detour-framing is itself the disease; full commitment is the virtue.

**Here is why this fails at the structural level.**

---

## c. Diagnosis of Monk A's failure

**Diagnosis 1: Monk A confuses ambition-to-define-scope with power-to-override-cryptographic-physics.** Louis's framing power lets him say "let's aim for 1B." It does not close a 3–5 order-of-magnitude gap between Kaizen (10M params, 2500% overhead, 8× Xeon Platinum, 512GB RAM) and frontier-scale training. [2] The zkLLM authors — cryptographers inside the work — said extending ZKP to LLM training "may pose insurmountable challenges." Gensyn publicly abandoned pure ZKP for refereed delegation because "cryptographic proofs are prohibitively expensive for large-scale ML." [3] Pierre's 10% overhead is 2+ orders of magnitude outside the published envelope, unverifiable from Louis's position, and the load-bearing premise of Monk A's case. If it is hybrid-protocol sleight-of-hand (TEE doing the work, ZKP providing selective attestation on a small surface), the distinctive ZKP credential evaporates — the actual engineering is TEE integration, not what the alignment community treats as cryptographic verification. If it is aspirational, the headline deliverable is structurally undeliverable. Louis cannot verify which without signing, and **signing is the expensive move.**

**Diagnosis 2: Monk A confuses "rare credential" with "credential the market Louis wants to reach rewards."** Suppose the project succeeds at its most ambitious framing: first-of-kind multi-GPU ZKP demo on 100M params, co-authored paper, open-source release. This opens Pattern 2 destinations — METR, FAR.AI, UK AISI, US AISI, Anthropic's *policy* team. These are **also** where the absence of frontier-engineering authority is the primary structural ceiling on effectiveness. Christiano's leverage at US AISI sits on his RLHF authorship; Leung's on her OpenAI policy-plus-technical history; Irving's on DeepMind debate work. Louis would arrive with a governance-engineering credential and none of the frontier-paradigm authority that makes senior policy voices listened-to. **The ZKP demo is a ticket to the second row. The frontier-engineering track is the ticket to the first row, redirectable into governance later.**

**Diagnosis 3: Monk A reads field-survey silence as neglected-that-rewards-entry.** The honest reading is **premature-neglect-that-won't-be-fillable-without-cryptographic-breakthroughs-years-away.** The Alignment Field Survey doesn't mention ZKP because the community has implicitly concluded frontier training verification is not tractable, and the tractable alternatives (TEE, behavioral evaluation, compute governance) are where attention belongs. flexHEG targets 2027 for hardware-enabled verification. [4] Gensyn's abandonment, zkLLM's pessimism, Kaizen's 2500% overhead — all point to noticed-and-correctly-shelved. Being first in a subfield that gets bypassed is not leverage; it is archaeology.

## d. The deeper principle at stake

**Principle 1: Technical authority is path-dependent and career-window-bound.** The Christiano/Amodei/Olah/Leike pattern is **frontier-first-then-authority-redirected**, with zero counter-examples at the top of the influence distribution. Louis's "rockstar exception" is the most common self-deception at this decision point — because the ambivalence it resolves is so seductive. Louis wants a story in which the guardian-move reinforces the builder-identity. Every person who tried this exact move — 12-month governance detour with planned frontier return — stayed. Governance ecosystems reward absorbed people with socially legible, values-aligned, well-paid senior roles structurally unavailable at the frontier, and the social loop closes. **Louis will not be the exception. Nobody has been.**

**Principle 2: Comparative advantage is measured against where the field is moving, not where it is now.** The 2026 inflection is in **agentic systems, RL post-training, long-horizon agents, test-time compute, memory-augmented architectures.** [5] Anthropic Fellows 2026 explicitly scopes into agentic misalignment, scalable oversight of autonomous systems, model organisms, adversarial robustness against self-improving agents. [6] The Frontier Red Team (Autonomy) and (Cyber) are hiring now. This is the paradigm where Louis's current enthusiasm — Claude Code, agentic stacks, the §6 ode in his presentation — signals where his tacit intuition is already forming. A 12-month ZKP-circuit immersion is a retreat from the paradigm where Louis's advantage compounds fastest. METR's time-horizon metric doubles every 4–7 months; 12 months out is 2–3 paradigm generations missed. The ZKP subfield is stable-to-regressive: Kaizen 2024, zkGPT 2025, no 2026 paper has closed the gap. **Louis is looking at the wrong frontier.**

**Corollary: relational decisions disguised as strategic decisions produce the worst long-run regret.** Louis weights Pierre heavily — *"someone whose judgment I deeply trust"* and *"it's my friends behind."* Both true, both load-bearing, both a known anti-pattern. The presentation is written for Pierre and Tom after an argument Louis lost orally; its confidence is RLHF-like social coherence with peers whose respect is at stake, not independent strategic conviction. Visible in the structure — answering Tom's critiques point-by-point rather than asking the prior question of whether Louis's advantage is frontier IC or governance-hybrid. When the guardian voice subsumes the builder voice inside a document addressed to the people who made the guardian voice louder, the document is not a strategic artifact. It is a harmony-preservation ritual.

## e. Push to the extreme

**Extreme 1: Louis should decline the grant outright.** Not negotiate higher ambition. Not scope the engagement. Not request publication guarantees and a 30% personal-project stream. **Decline.** Once inside GPAI, the social gravity of Pierre's mission, the paper timeline, and policy-relevance will make exit at month 9 a real act of repudiation, not a planned off-ramp. The exit door is a rhetorical device, not a lived option. The only moment at which declining costs Louis no social capital is *now*. Tell Pierre: *the work matters, and I will be maximally useful to it from a position of frontier-engineering authority in 3–5 years, not as an early partner now.*

**Extreme 2: Pursue Anthropic aggressively right now.** Not Kyutai. Not Pruna. Not a French scale-up. **Anthropic**, because it is the one lab where frontier-capability and safety-of-frontier-models are *already unified inside the same institution* — which is what Louis is trying to get GPAI to be and GPAI structurally cannot be. GPAI has no training infrastructure, no frontier models, no direct capability leverage, no ability to intervene at the moment of paradigm formation. Anthropic has all four. The Fellows program starts May/July 2026 with applications due April 26, 2026 [7] — **the deadline is two weeks away and the program targets exactly Louis's skill stack.** Kyutai is the fallback (French frontier training, preserves agentic paradigm exposure), not GPAI.

**Extreme 3: the distinctive credential Louis imagines is a mirage.** Either the project hits the physics wall (10–100M demo, "we aimed high" narrative, weaker than a scoped success) or succeeds via hybrid TEE-commitment protocols where the actual contribution is TEE integration, not a ZKP credential. The real distinctive credential for a Louis-shaped person: **"ML engineer who led agentic / RL-post-training work at a frontier lab 2026–2029, published on scalable oversight of autonomous agents, then redirected verification-infrastructure research from unquestionable technical authority in 2030."** That path produces Christianos, Amodeis, Leikes. GPAI-first, even executed brilliantly, produces Leung-level governance careers — excellent, values-aligned, structurally capped.

## f. Reasoning skeleton

**Premises:**
1. Louis's testimony shows a builder identity (§2.4 items 1 and 7, §6 of presentation, agentic enthusiasm, 42AI history).
2. The 2026 shift is agentic / RL-post-training / long-horizon agents — frontier labs are hiring for exactly this.
3. ZKP-of-training has a 3–5 order-of-magnitude gap to frontier-scale, widely acknowledged by cryptographers inside the work.
4. Career-trajectory data shows zero governance-to-frontier crossings.
5. Louis's presentation is written for Pierre and Tom after a debate lost orally — argumentative, not introspective.

**Inference steps:** Formation-window logic → tacit intuition only accretes inside the paradigm → 12 months out = 2–3 paradigm shifts missed. Authority-redirection logic → every high-leverage safety voice was frontier-formed first. Load-bearing-friendship logic → Pierre-weight is large enough the decision is not on honest comparative-advantage grounds. Physics-over-ambition logic → Louis's framing power does not close cryptographic overhead gaps.

**Load-bearing claims (if these fall, the argument collapses):**
- **LB1:** 2026 inflection is specifically in agentic / RL-post-training / long-horizon agents; frontier labs are where the paradigm is forming.
- **LB2:** Pierre's 10% overhead is unverifiable pre-signing; signing is the expensive move.
- **LB3:** Career-trajectory data holds: zero governance-to-frontier crossings.
- **LB4:** Anthropic or equivalent is accessible within a 90-day window.
- **LB5:** The distinctive-credential theory does not produce Pattern 1 destinations.

**Bets:** LB1 and LB3 are firmest. LB2 is firm enough the burden of proof is on Pierre. LB4 is most contingent — if Anthropic/Kyutai don't materialize in 90 days, the argument needs a bridge plan (targeted upskilling, aggressive applications, *no* GPAI commitment interim). LB5 is where I have least certainty, but Louis's own testimony — "I'm still learning a lot on SOTA LLM trainings" and the §6 builder-ode — suggests his values-preferred endpoint is closer to Pattern 1 than he admits.
