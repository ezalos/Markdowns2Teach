## Main Application Essays

**22\. Proposal** *(250 words maximum)*

I propose a 6-month career transition to work with the GPAI Policy Lab on demonstrating Zero-Knowledge Proof (ZKP) verification of large AI training runs under real-world conditions.

**The problem:** As AI capabilities advance, international governance frameworks need tools to verify that frontier labs comply with safety requirements — without exposing proprietary model details. ZKPs are theoretically ideal for this: they let a lab prove properties of a training run (compute used, data constraints, safety procedures) while revealing nothing else. However, current ZKP implementations work for inference but do not scale to frontier training. This gap between policy need and technical capability is the central obstacle to enforceable AI governance agreements.

**The activity:** Working under Pierre Peigne at GPAI Policy Lab, I will help bridge this gap by bringing large-scale ML engineering expertise to ZKP training verification research. My 5+ years building production ML systems — billion-scale embedding pipelines on Kubernetes, distributed multi-GPU training, 50k-line codebase architectures — are directly relevant to understanding what training verification must handle in practice. The goal is to produce a concrete demonstration of ZKP-verified training properties on a meaningful-scale run, advancing the field from theoretical frameworks to practical feasibility.

**Career path and impact:** This positions me at the intersection of AI safety governance and technical implementation — a space with critical talent shortage. The resulting career paths include compute governance roles at frontier labs, technical AI policy positions, or continuing research in verification methods. Ultimately, verifiable AI training is foundational infrastructure for any international AI safety framework: without verification, agreements on compute limits or safety training requirements remain unenforceable.
