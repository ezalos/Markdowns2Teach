## Main Application Essays

**22\. Proposal** *(250 words maximum)*

I propose a 6-to-9-month career transition (ideally 9 months) to work with the GPAI Policy Lab on demonstrating Zero-Knowledge Proof (ZKP) verification of large AI training runs under real-world conditions.

**The problem:** As AI capabilities advance toward AGI, international governance frameworks need tools to verify that frontier labs comply with safety requirements — without exposing proprietary model details. The core challenge is a game-theoretic trap: every nation is incentivized to race on capabilities because unilateral restraint doesn't eliminate risk from others. ZKPs can break this deadlock by enabling verifiable compliance — transforming a prisoner's dilemma into a coordination game. However, current ZKP implementations handle inference but do not scale to frontier training. This gap between policy need and technical capability is the central obstacle to enforceable AI governance.

**The activity:** Working under Pierre Peigne at GPAI Policy Lab, I will bring large-scale ML engineering expertise to ZKP training verification research. My 5+ years building production ML systems — billion-scale embedding pipelines on Kubernetes, distributed multi-GPU training, a 15,000-line TensorFlow-to-PyTorch migration achieving ~10% performance gains, and 133 ablation studies across 30+ models for agent evaluation — are directly relevant to understanding what training verification must handle in practice. The goal is a concrete demonstration of ZKP-verified training properties on a meaningful-scale run.

**Career path and impact:** This positions me at the intersection of AI safety governance and technical implementation — a space with critical talent shortage. Verifiable AI training is foundational infrastructure for any international safety framework: without verification, agreements on compute limits or safety requirements remain unenforceable.
