# Research brief — AEO: the untapped alpha, beyond one anecdote

## Question
How large and how real is AI-referral traffic (ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity → your product) in 2026, and what do documented AEO practices + scheduled-agent automations actually look like — beyond swyx's self-reported numbers?

## Why it matters for the talk
This is the most directly actionable "do this next week" card for founders: a scheduled agent loop (autoresearch your AEO weekly) that costs nothing to try. But the only source is swyx's tweet ("AEO will be responsible for $1M of my revenue next year", ~300 ChatGPT-referred ticket buyers) — a founder anecdote, citable only as opinion. To teach it with leverage we need measured data.

## What we already have
- `sources/tweet-2078244735794413786-swyx-seo-automation.md` — full 4-tweet capture: schedule {Codex|Claude|Gemini|Devin} automations for weekly SEO/AEO autoresearch; the "on-policy autoaeo" question (does Claude optimizing your AEO disproportionately convert Claude users?); UTM spreadsheet screenshot (AI Engineer WF ticket sales).

## What to find
1. **Traffic data**: published measurements of AI-assistant referral traffic share and growth in 2026 (Cloudflare Radar, Similarweb, Adobe Analytics holiday-traffic reports, Profound/analytics vendors) — the citable curve "AI referrals are ×N y/y".
2. **Conversion quality**: any study showing AI-referred visitors convert better/worse than search-referred (Adobe and several analytics firms published this — get the primary).
3. **What AEO concretely is**: the 3-5 practices with evidence (llms.txt adoption status and whether any assistant actually reads it; structured data; being cited in training-data-era content vs retrieval-era content; Reddit/docs presence). Separate measured effect from folklore.
4. **The automation pattern**: documented examples of scheduled agent automations doing this (Claude Code automations/cron, Codex scheduled tasks) — any public write-up with outcomes.
5. **The on-policy question**: any evidence models disproportionately recommend content optimized on themselves (studies on self-preference bias in recommendations) — swyx's open question, answered or honestly marked open.

## Acceptance
One citable traffic-growth number + one conversion-quality number (exact URLs + verbatim quotes), a 3-item evidence-based practice list, and swyx's $1M clearly framed as a practitioner anecdote on top of measured data.

## Method
One deep-research run; prefer analytics-infrastructure primaries (Cloudflare, Adobe, Similarweb) over SEO-vendor content marketing.
