### LeCun's actual "roadmap" (the substance Louis wants)

- **Diagnosis**: LLMs manipulate language and "fool us into thinking they are smart", but are "completely helpless when it comes to the physical world"; agentic systems act without being able to predict the consequences of their actions. Scaling LLMs will not reach human-level intelligence — a dead end for AGI.
- **Alternative**: **world models** — predictive systems: given the current world state + an imagined action, predict the next state; that prediction enables planning and reasoning. Trained on noisy multimodal data (video, images, audio, sensor/scientific data), not just text.
- **Architecture**: **JEPA** (Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture) — predict in abstract representation space rather than pixel/token space (V-JEPA line of work at Meta preceded this).
- **Vehicle**: AMI Labs (Paris), pursuing "a new breed of AI systems that understand the world, can reason and plan, and are controllable and safe."
- **Canonical same-title primary recording**: "Yann LeCun: World Models: Enabling the next AI revolution", ETH Zürich (Computer Vision and Geometry Group channel), uploaded 2026-06-09, 59 min, ~226k views — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=72Xj8k5WQX4 (verified via yt-dlp).
