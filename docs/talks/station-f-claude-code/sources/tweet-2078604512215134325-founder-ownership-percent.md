### The math levelsio runs (summary)

Starting from Carta medians for a SINGLE founder (Seed ~56%, A ~36%, B ~23.5%, C ~16.5%, D ~10%, E <10%), he divides by co-founder count (2 or 4), then multiplies by exit sizes: (a) a $1B exit, (b) the "realistic" $71M median exit, and (c) finally weights by timing (median exit ~Series A) and probability (~15% of startups ever get acquired) to land on **expected outcomes: solo founder ~$3.84M · one of 2 co-founders ~$1.92M · one of 4 co-founders ~$960k**. He flags what's excluded: taxes and liquidation preferences (which reduce founder take) vs secondaries (which increase it), claiming they roughly cancel. Closing stance: this is just how the VC game works — VC is for shooting for the moon; billionaire outcomes essentially never happen bootstrapped.
