## Linked content summaries

**The State of the AI Economy** (Exponential View, 2026-06-25, https://www.exponentialview.co/p/the-state-of-the-ai-economy, portal: https://intelligence.exponentialview.co/):
First bottom-up, deduplicated measure of consumer + enterprise GenAI spend across the full stack (apps, foundation models, infra hosting; global ex-China; excludes chips and professional services). Authors: Azeem Azhar, William Gildea, Nathan Warren et al. Methodology: reconstructed P&Ls for hyperscalers (Amazon, Google, Microsoft) and specialist AI clouds, public disclosures + leaks + self-reports with confidence scoring, triangulation, dedup to end-customer spend only. Headlines: $110bn trailing-12-month revenue, $175bn annualized run rate, adoption ~3x faster than internet/mobile waves, ~$2T CapEx committed through 2026 (increasingly debt-funded; as of Q4 2025 AI revenue barely covers depreciation — 6-year compute life assumption), 30T tokens/month (14x YoY), >$7bn revenue per GW of DC capacity. Demand elasticity: every 10% price cut yields 12–18% more token consumption. Contains four growth scenarios and a "quality-adjusted tokens" framework. Corroborated by Bloomberg coverage the same day (link above).
